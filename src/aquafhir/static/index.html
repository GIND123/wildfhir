Overview

A One Health data bus for water and Earth-observation readings. Every value is proposed by rules or by Gemini, approved by a human, published as FHIR R4, evaluated against a versioned threshold policy, and hash-chained end to end.

Data-bus pipeline

Every arrow crosses a boundary that is logged and verifiable. The human is not a rubber stamp — it is a hard gate before anything reaches FHIR or the alert router.

    Design principles

    What a judge should be able to verify in 60 seconds.

    01
    Nothing publishes without a human.  requires_review is unconditional. No confidence score bypasses it.
    02
    Gemini never decides the number.  Unit conversion factors live in curated YAML. The model maps labels, not magnitudes.
    03
    Closed vocabulary.  The model can only return codes that exist in the OneAquaHealth catalog.
    04
    Alerting is deterministic.  Thresholds are versioned YAML. No model reasons about safety.
    05
    Every step hash-chained.  Prompt hashes, response hashes, latencies, reviewer identity — all replayable.

    Review queue

    Nothing reaches the FHIR server until someone here approves it. Curated-rule proposals and Gemini-assisted proposals arrive in the same queue and cross the same gate.

    J/K move · A approve · R reject · T terminology

    Ingest

    Two doors into the same review queue: a typed reading, or a bulletin someone pasted out of an email. Measurements not literally in the text become warnings, never inferences.

    Single reading

    Structured input from an agency feed, a citizen app, or a satellite index.

    The default is a German field label with a non-UCUM unit — curated aliases alone cannot resolve it.

    Unstructured note Gemini

    Free text is read by Gemini and extracted literally — nothing is inferred.

    Measurements that aren't written down become warnings.

    Sites

    Monitoring stations, plotted from the lat/lon on approved readings. Colour is severity of the most recent alert. No tile layer — the sketch is drawn from the data.

    Basin sketch

    Simplified geometry drawn to fit the observed lat/lon extent.

    Alerts

    Raised by the versioned threshold policy, not by a model. Advisories are drafts for an accountable authority to review.

    Alert timeline

    Alerts by observation time, coloured by severity.

    Audit chain

    Every proposal, decision, alert, and model call is SHA-256 hash-chained. Editing a stored row breaks verification at that sequence and every one after it.

    Chain visualization

    Each block is one hashed event. Colour is the event type.
