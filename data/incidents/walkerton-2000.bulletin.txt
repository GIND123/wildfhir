Bruce-Grey-Owen Sound Health Unit — retrospective reconstruction
17 May 2000 — Walkerton, Ontario.

Well 5 free chlorine residual recorded as 0.00 mg/L on 15 May.
Total coliforms 2400 CFU/100 mL at the well head on 15 May.
Faecal coliforms 1600 CFU/100 mL in the distribution system on 17 May.
Ammonium 1.8 mg/L. Total suspended solids 42 mg/L following heavy rain 12 May.

The laboratory result was communicated to the utility but not to the health
unit. No boil water advisory was in force on 17 May.
