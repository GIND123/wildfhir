South West Water / UKHSA South West — incident note
15 May 2024, 09:00 BST — Hillhead service reservoir, Brixham.

Residents in Alston and Hillhead reported gastrointestinal illness from
14 May. Total coliforms at the reservoir 1250 CFU/100 mL. Free chlorine
residual 0.06 mg/L. Total suspended solids 38 mg/L on 14 May.

Boil water notice issued to approximately 16,000 properties.

Cryptosporidium oocysts confirmed by the laboratory — count not yet reported.
